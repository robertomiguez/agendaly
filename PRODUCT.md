# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Agendaly serves independent beauty and wellness professionals, barbershops, salons, growing multi-location service businesses, and the customers booking appointments with them. Providers use it to manage services, staff, locations, availability, subscriptions, and calendars. Customers use it from provider pages or direct links to book quickly, confirm details, and return to their appointments.

## Product Purpose

Agendaly exists to replace scattered appointment coordination with a clear, reliable scheduling system. It helps service providers publish a business page, share booking links, control availability, manage daily operations, and reduce manual back-and-forth. Success means customers can finish a booking without confusion, while providers can run their schedule with less interruption and more confidence.

## Brand Personality

Capable, calm, quietly premium. The product should feel practical and trustworthy without becoming cold. It should be polished enough for serious businesses, simple enough for independent professionals, and restrained enough to let each provider's own identity come through.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, overdecorated salon aesthetics, sterile enterprise scheduling tools, noisy marketplace vibes, and anything that feels like WhatsApp chaos translated into software. Avoid visual clutter, decorative metrics, and patterns that make the provider feel like they are configuring software instead of running their business.

## Design Principles

- Make booking feel inevitable: fewer decisions, clear next steps, no dead ends.
- Respect provider time: operational screens should be dense enough for daily use, but never cluttered.
- Let each business feel like itself: Agendaly should support provider identity without becoming a marketplace.
- Prefer trust over flash: reliable, readable, polished interactions beat decorative novelty.
- Mobile first where customers book, desktop efficient where providers manage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Prioritize readable contrast, clear focus states, reliable keyboard access, reduced-motion support, and touch targets that work comfortably on mobile booking flows. Copy should stay plain and direct for bilingual English and Portuguese users.
